How it works

  1. Photo / PDF: Take a photo of a textbook, notebook, or handout, or choose a photo or PDF. With a PDF you pick the pages to import. New here? Start with “Try a sample” or “Watch the demo” — there are four sample pages to pick from (science, history, math, and civics).
  2. Mark: Run your finger over the terms you want to remember (any marker color). Underlines and circles drawn with the pencil count too. Pinch to zoom or pan. “Spots” shows exactly what will be quizzed.
  3. Pick a difficulty (Easy, Medium, or Hard) and tap “Make a quiz.”
  4. Terms: Type the hidden term to get text choices, or skip this step to answer with image choices only.
  5. Quiz: Choose which of the four options belongs under the red sheet.
  6. Results: Review your answers, then retry just the ones you missed or move up a level.

Add it to your home screen

  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then “Add to Home Screen.”
  • Android (Chrome): open the menu (⋮), then “Add to Home screen” or “Install app.”
  • It also works if you save this file to your device (Files, Google Drive, and so on) and open it from there.

About PDFs

  • Each PDF page is converted to an image (long edge 1600 px) and imported. If you pick several pages, each one becomes its own item in History, so you can quiz yourself page by page.
  • PDFs are rendered with Mozilla’s pdf.js (Apache License 2.0). It is downloaded the first time you open a PDF, so you need an internet connection just once. The PDF itself never leaves your device.
  • For fully offline use, place pdf.min.js and pdf.worker.min.js (from pdfjs-dist 3.11.174, legacy/build) in the same folder as this HTML file. Adding the cmaps/ and standard_fonts/ folders as well helps with PDFs that don’t embed their fonts.
  • Password-protected PDFs ask for the password when you open them.
  • On a phone, pick PDFs from the Files app (iPhone) or from Files or Drive (Android).

Your data

  • Photos, marks, terms, and scores are stored only in this browser on this device (IndexedDB). Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Photos and PDF pages are saved as JPEGs with a long edge of 1600 px. From History you can delete items one by one or export and import them as JSON.
  • Clearing your browser’s site data deletes this data too. Export anything you want to keep.

About the backend

This single HTML file separates the frontend (screens and drawing) from the backend (storage, spot detection, quiz generation, and scoring). The backend runs on your device as the LocalBackend class and never uploads your photos. Swapping in the RemoteBackend stub, which has the same API, would turn it into a cloud-synced edition.
